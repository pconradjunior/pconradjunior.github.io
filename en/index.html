About

Between 2002 and 2009 I taught in Technical Education and Higher Education. Since 2010 I have been an IT analyst at Universidade Federal do Pampa (Unipampa), with over 20 years of experience building institutional systems and mobile apps.

I work on software architecture, data modeling, relational databases and technical leadership. During that period, between 2016 and 2019, I coordinated technical teams.

Skills

From requirements gathering to delivering institutional systems, I work across software architecture, data modeling and secure development. I share that experience through technical leadership, team training, mentoring and agile management. Study and certifications complement the work with CI, Firebase, Docker, Power BI, IT risk, artificial intelligence, LLMs and prompt engineering.

Architecture, engineering and AI

An experimental software engineering initiative: treating the process itself as code, with explicit phases, structured knowledge and human validation before any delivery.

Specialized agents

Each phase has a responsible agent, with a defined input, output and stopping criterion.

Structured knowledge

Rules, routes and technical references are versioned and searchable instead of relying on informal memory.

Traceability

Decisions and open items are recorded so a session can resume without losing context.

Human validation

Formal gates and critical decisions require approval; automation does not replace the requester's judgment.
