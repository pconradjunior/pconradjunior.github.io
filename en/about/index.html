About

I started computing in 1996 and have worked with software ever since, which adds up to over 30 years. I work as an Information Technology Analyst at Universidade Federal do Pampa (Unipampa), contributing directly to critical institutional systems.

I have strong experience in software architecture, data modeling, and development standards definition. Between 2016 and 2019, I coordinated development teams, leading projects, organizing processes, and mentoring developers.

My background includes large-scale systems, such as an ERP with over 50 integrated modules, as well as modern mobile applications using Flutter. I started on the desktop with Visual Basic and Delphi, moved on to C#, and became a reference in web development with PHP and CodeIgniter.
