Resume

I focus my work on Information Systems and I am always looking for new challenges. I have been working with computers since 1996, when I was just building systems to learn something new.

Speaking of systems development, I worked on desktop projects in the early 2000s with Visual Basic and Delphi and, more recently, with C#. I built systems for web browsers with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and PHP, and I work with Flutter focused on mobile devices.

I also work managing teams, helping define development processes and software architecture, modeling databases, creating and reviewing technical and user documentation, evaluating tools and reviewing code.

Throughout my journey I can safely say that I consider myself an 'eternal apprentice', as I am always learning from organizations, their culture, technology and mainly, from people.
